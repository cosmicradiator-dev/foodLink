ABOUT US

FoodLink connects people who have extra edible food (hostel messes, canteens, fests) with people who can use it before it spoils.

How it works

  1. A donor adds the surplus food with its expiry time.
  2. A inspector checks the food physically and gives a approval for ngos to collect them.
  3. A ngo claims it and gets a 4 digit pickup code.
  4. They collect it and show the code to the donor.
  5. The donor enters the code and the meals are counted as saved.
